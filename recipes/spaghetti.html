Spaghetti Recipe

Description

Spaghetti is a long, thin, solid pasta made from durum wheat or semolina flour and water. It's a staple of Italian cuisine and is often served with tomato sauce, meatballs, or vegetables.

The word spaghetti comes from the Italian word spaghetto, which is a diminutive of spago meaning "thin string" or "twine"

Ingredients

  • spaghetti
  • tomato sauce
  • garlic
  • onion
  • olive oil
