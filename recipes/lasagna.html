Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is an Italian dish made of flat pasta sheets layered with fillings and baked. The word lasagna can refer to the pasta sheets or the dish itself.

Lasagna originated in Italy during the middle ages, and has been attributed to the city of Naples

Ingredients

  • Pasta sheets
  • Ragù (ground meat and tomato sauce)
  • Béchamel sauce
  • Vegetables
  • Cheese, such as mozzarella, ricotta, or Parmesan
  • Seasonings and spices
